## 🧠 Overview

**DAYFLOW AI** is an intelligent **Odoo 17 HR workforce intelligence platform** that extends traditional HR management with explainable workforce risk analysis, proactive HR alerts, department intelligence, smart leave impact analysis, attendance trend analysis, and an AI HR Copilot.

Traditional HRMS systems mainly store and display HR information.

DAYFLOW AI goes further by analyzing workforce data and helping HR understand:

- What is happening?
- Why is it happening?
- Which employees or departments need attention?
- What action should HR consider next?

---

## 🎯 Problem

Traditional HR systems manage:

- Employee records
- Attendance
- Leave
- Payroll
- Reports

However, HR teams often need to manually analyze this information to identify:

- Increasing absenteeism
- Declining attendance
- Repeated late check-ins
- High-risk employees
- Low department availability
- Leave conflicts
- Workforce capacity issues

This creates a gap between **HR data** and **HR decision-making**.
